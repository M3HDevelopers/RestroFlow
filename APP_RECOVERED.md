### 📝 HOW TO USE:

1. **Login:**
   - Email: admin@restroflow.pk
   - Password: admin123
   - Click "Sign In"

2. **Create Order:**
   - Go to "Point of Sale"
   - Select order type (Dine-in/Takeaway/Delivery)
   - Add items from menu
   - Select table (dine-in) or customer (delivery)
   - Apply discount if needed
   - Click "Pay"
   - Select payment method
   - Complete order

3. **View Kitchen:**
   - Go to "Kitchen Display"
   - See real-time orders from POS
   - Accept, mark ready, or complete orders

4. **Manage Menu:**
   - Go to "Menu Management"
   - Add, edit, or delete items
   - Upload images
   - Toggle availability

5. **Manage Inventory:**
   - Go to "Inventory"
   - Add, edit, or delete items
   - Update stock levels
   - View low stock alerts
